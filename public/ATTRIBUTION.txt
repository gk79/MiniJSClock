City names, country codes, population, and time-zone mappings used in the
MiniJSClock city catalog are derived from GeoNames data:
https://www.geonames.org/

GeoNames data is made available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The data was transformed into a normalized input and curated 400-city subset.
Source snapshot identity and transformation details are recorded in the
MiniJSClock repository under data/geonames/.
